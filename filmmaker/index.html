I Can Be Nothing

Director, Screenwriter, Editor, Colorist

About
Genre
Experimental, Drama
Runtime
06:58
Year
2024

My first short film — a no-budget production made with the help of my friends. Set in a modern world where most people try to do everything at once, it follows 23-year-old Alisa, who struggles with anxiety, and her twin, who begins to notice it. Together they go through the process of understanding it.

03

Essence of a Tree

Director, Screenwriter, Director of Photography, Editor, Colorist, Sound

About
Genre
Experimental
Runtime
01:09
Year
2024

A sensory journey into the essence of a tree. The film follows the life cycle from roots to branches, using metaphorical imagery to reveal the invisible connections between nature and humanity. Blending image and sound, it portrays not only the physical presence of a tree but also its spiritual and symbolic bond with nature.
